ABOUT THE TREATMENT

Fresh skin starts with proper care.

What is Hydra Facial?

Hydra Facial is a skin care treatment focused on deep cleansing and hydration. It is designed to refresh the appearance of the skin while helping remove surface impurities.

Who is it for?

It may be suitable for people concerned about dull, dry, dehydrated or congested-looking skin. Your skin is assessed before treatment so the approach can be personalised to your needs.

Key Benefits

✓ Deep cleansing
✓ Skin hydration
✓ Fresh-looking skin
✓ Improved skin radiance
