OUR ACHIEVEMENTS

Achievements & Recognition

Milestones, recognitions and professional achievements of Marmm.

ACHIEVEMENT 01

International Recognition

The World FUE Institute is a leading global organization dedicated to advancing hair restoration through Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE), bringing together top experts worldwide to set the highest standards in the field. Alongside the ISHRS, WFI is one of the world's most recognized societies for hair are often advised to check for accreditation from bodies like WFI or ISHRS as a marker of safety and quality.

ACHIEVEMENT 02

Professional Excellence

Dr. Amit Porwal's contribution to the field has been recognized beyond the operating room. He was awarded "Best Surgeon in the Cosmetic & Hair Transplant Sector" by The Times of India, in recognition of his sustained excellence in patient care and surgical outcomes.

ACHIEVEMENT 03

Advanced Training

Beyond his own practice, Dr. Amit Porwal is regularly invited to mentor and train the next generation of cosmetic and hair transplant surgeons. He has delivered guest lectures at respected international platforms including the Faces Team International Congress, Brazil, at the Society of Hair Transplant Surgeons (SHTS), India, and at some of India's most reputed medical colleges — sharing his surgical experience with young surgeons entering the field.

ACHIEVEMENT 04

Milestones & Awards

Dr. Amit Porwal's work has also been recognized closer to home — by Dainik Bhaskar and the My FM Merit Award, both honoring his contribution to healthcare in Indore, and through recognition for innovation in hair transplant technique from Merit Awards & Market Research, acknowledging his role in advancing surgical methods in the field.
