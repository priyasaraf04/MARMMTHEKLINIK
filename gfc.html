GFC HAIR THERAPY

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) Therapy

GFC is an advanced non-surgical hair restoration therapy that uses concentrated growth factors derived from your own blood. These growth factors are carefully prepared and delivered to the scalp to support hair follicles, reduce hair fall and encourage healthier hair growth.

BLOOD COLLECTION

Around 8-16 ml of blood is drawn from the patient's arm and collected in specialized GFC tubes.

Activation

The tubes are left to stand for about 30 minutes. A special activator inside the tube breaks down the platelets to release the active "Growth Factors."

Centrifugation (Machine Process)

The tubes are spun in a centrifuge machine at high speeds. This separates the pure, liquid growth factors from the rest of the blood cells (RBCs/WBCs).

Extraction & Injection

The pure growth factor concentrate collected at the top of the tube is extracted into a syringe and carefully injected into the hair roots (scalp).

EXOSOME HAIR THERAPY

Exosome Therapy for Hair Restoration

Exosome therapy is an advanced hair restoration approach that uses nano-sized extracellular vesicles containing proteins, growth factors and other signaling molecules. These signals may help support communication between cells and create a healthier environment for hair follicles.

Consultation & Scalp Assessment

The scalp and hair-loss pattern are assessed to determine whether exosome therapy may be suitable for the patient.

Exosome Preparation

A clinically prepared exosome-based product containing signaling molecules and growth factors is prepared for treatment.

Scalp Preparation

The targeted scalp area is cleaned and prepared before the treatment is performed.

Application to Scalp

The exosome preparation is carefully delivered to the targeted scalp areas to support the hair follicles and their surrounding environment.
